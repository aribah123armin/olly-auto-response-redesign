# Product Problem : Limited edits in Auto-response

**About Olly**

Olly is an AI-powered online reputation management platform built for multi-location restaurant brands. It pulls reviews from across platforms, Zomato, Google, Swiggy, TripAdvisor, into a single dashboard, automatically extracts complaints and praises, and generates AI responses to reviews in the brand's voice. The goal: help restaurant teams stay on top of their reputation without spending hours manually managing feedback across platforms.

**Context**

One of Olly's core features is Automated AI Responses, Olly learns your brand's tone and replies to reviews on your behalf. Before activating full auto-response, users go through a setup flow where they can review and tweak the AI-generated response before it goes live.

**The Problem**

The editing experience in this setup flow is limited to a fixed set of preset action chips, *Regenerate, Enhance it, Make it friendlier, Make it professional, Make it more casual, Add or Remove contact info.* There's no way to make freeform edits. If the response is 80% right but needs one specific change, a different word, an added detail, added context of the user’s brand, a line they want rewritten, a line they always want to add at the end of the response to promote something, users want to give out an offer to people who leave negative reviews to bring them back, but they simply can't do it. They're stuck choosing from buttons that may not match what they actually need.

**Your Challenge**

How would you redesign this editing experience so users feel genuinely in control of the response, without making the flow feel heavy or slowing them down? Think about: what's the right level of control here? How do you balance flexibility with the speed that auto-response is supposed to deliver? If the setup is very lengthy, there’s a high chance of users dropping off without getting the value and later churn from the app
